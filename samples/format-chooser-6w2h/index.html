Rise, Storyline, or a job aid?

Picking a format is a design decision, not a software preference. Rise 360 builds clean, responsive courses quickly. Storyline 360 builds custom practice, such as software simulations and branching scenarios. A job aid puts the steps in front of people while they work, so they do not have to remember them.

Answer eight questions about the task, the people, and the setting. We score each answer toward the formats it favors, apply a few firm rules that the research supports, and show you the pick with the reasons and sources behind it.

How this tool decides

Each answer adds points to the formats it favors. The format with the most points is the starting pick. The point values are our judgment. On the first question you can choose more than one goal. We average the points of the goals you choose, so that question never outweighs the others, and every firm rule still applies to each goal. That averaging is our choice.

Four firm rules come from the research and beat the count:

  • Software practice, complex judgment practice, or advanced tracking means the pick includes Storyline. On a phone, that becomes a Storyline block inside Rise.
  • When people cannot read a guide in time, or the LMS must track the training, a job aid cannot be the whole answer.
  • Costly mistakes mean the pick includes a checklist.
  • Rare tasks mean the pick includes a job aid, because skills fade when they go unused.

When two formats tie, we pick the simpler build: a job aid, then Rise, then Storyline. That tie rule is our choice, not a research finding.

We quote Articulate on accessibility rather than calling any tool compliant. How accessible a course is depends on how it is built.
