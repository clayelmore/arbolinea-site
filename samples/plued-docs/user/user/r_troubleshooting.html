    Troubleshooting

    This table lists problems cashiers and shoppers report, what causes them and how to fix them. Quoted text is what the app shows.

    Table 1. Problems and fixes
    ProblemCauseFix
    The unlock sheet shows Store unavailable. The app store did not answer within about 10 seconds. Check your connection and tap Retry.
    You bought the full app, but this device is locked. The purchase was made on another device. Tap Restore purchases in Settings, signed in to the same app store account.
    A pack link opens a web page and not the app. The link was opened on a device without the app, or the device did not hand it to the app. Copy the link. In the app, open Import Store Pack, paste the link and tap Import.
    Could not reach the pack. Check your connection. A pack link or QR code needs a connection the first time. Connect and import again, or ask your trainer for the .plupack file.
    This pack is no longer available. The pack was removed. Ask your trainer for a new link.
    Camera scanning isn’t available here. Paste the link. The device has no camera the app can use. Paste the pack link in Import Store Pack.
    The sticker scanner shows No code found yet. The sticker is too close, moving or in glare. Hold it about a hand's length away and keep it still. If it still fails, tap Type it instead.
    The scanner says a code is not in PLUed. The code is a store's own code. Import your store's pack, or tap Add "code" as a Store Code.
    A code you need is missing from a concept card. Your region setting hides the other region's codes. In Settings, under My region, tap your region or I'm not sure, show all codes.
    A code never comes up in flashcards or games. Its star is off, or it is a Retired or Non-US code. Open its concept card and tap the star on its row.
    The Weak chip shows Not enough data yet. Fewer than 10 answers are on record. Keep studying. Weak spots start after 10 answers.
    That code was not recognized. Check the spelling with your manager. The store code was typed wrong. Check the code with your manager, or tap Scan the poster.
    All of your store's seats are in use. Ask your manager to add more. Every seat on the store's plan is taken. Tap Tell your manager. The code works once the store adds seats.
