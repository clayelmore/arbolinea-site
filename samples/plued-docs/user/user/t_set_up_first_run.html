    Set up PLUed at first launch

    The first time you open the app, it asks whether you work a register or are shopping. Cashiers then pick a region and an experience level and can load their store's codes.

    About this task

    Every choice on these pages can change later in Settings. To leave the setup at any point, tap Skip. Skipping sets you up as a cashier with all regions shown.

    Procedure

    1. Optional: To change the language, tap EN or ES at the top right of the welcome page.
    2. Under What brings you to PLUed?, tap I work a register or I'm shopping.
    3. Tap Continue.
      If you chose I'm shopping, the app skips to the last page. Continue with step 8.
    4. Under Where do you ring?, tap East / Central, West or Show all.

      The app hides the codes that apply only in the other region. If you are not sure, tap Show all.

    5. Under How much produce have you rung?, tap New or Pro.

      This choice only suggests where to start. It does not lock anything.

    6. Under Got a Store Pack?, decide whether your store gave you anything.
      IfThen
      A trainer shared a QR code, a link or a file with the store's codes Tap Import now.
      Your store gave you a store code Tap I have a store code.
      You have neither Continue with step 7.
    7. Tap Get started.
    8. On Your stall is set, tap Enter the market.
      Home opens.

    What to do next

    Appearance and language follow your device. To change them, or to run this setup again, use Settings.
