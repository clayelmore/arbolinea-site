    Task

    Study with flashcards

    Each flashcard is one kind of produce, with a slot for each of its codes. You type the codes from memory, and the app brings each card back when you are about to forget it.

    Procedure

    1. At the bottom of the screen, tap Flashcards.
      The review deck opens. The front of a card asks What are the codes? and shows one empty slot for each code you study.
    2. Optional: Tap a chip to narrow the deck.
      ChipCards in the deck
      All Every card that is due for review.
      Starred Only the codes you keep starred.
      Weak The codes you keep missing. With fewer than 10 answers on record, it shows Not enough data yet.
    3. Type each code on the keypad.

      Each slot takes only its own code, or that code's regional twin. A slot you get wrong keeps your entry in red, with the correct code under it.

      The card turns over and shows all its codes.
    4. Swipe left for the next card.

      After the last card, the deck starts again from the first.

    Results

    If you do not know a card, tap it to turn it over before you answer. Then rate yourself Missed or Tough. A card you turned over first does not count toward Known.

    Note:
    The chips appear only on the deck you open from Flashcards. Flashcards opened from a concept card, a level or the Library study exactly that set.
