    Play Quick Play on the belt

    In Quick Play, produce rides a checkout belt and you type each code before the item reaches the end. You earn and lose stars as you go.

    Procedure

    1. On Home, on the Continue card, tap Quick Play.

      You can also start it from a level's sheet, or from the bar at the bottom of the Library, where it plays up to 20 of the items in view.

      A countdown runs, and the first item comes onto the belt.
    2. Type the item's code on the keypad.
      The app checks the answer when the last digit is in.
    3. If you are not sure of the item, tap its tag or the What Kind? pill under it.

      The app names the item and shows its details, without the code. A correct answer after this earns no stars.

    4. If you do not know the code, tap Lookup.

      Lookup costs a star and shows the code. The belt stops.

    5. Type the code that Lookup showed.

      The prompt says Retype this code. The game does not go on until you type the exact code. The retype earns no stars.

    6. If a coaching sheet opens after a wrong answer, tap Got it!.

      The sheet shows your code beside the right one. To compare the two items, tap See them side by side.

      Note:
      Each code is scored on its own. A code for another size, growing method or form of the same produce counts as a miss. A code that differs only by region is accepted when the two codes are listed as regional twins.
    7. When your stars run out, work through the Break Time flashcards.

      Break Time shows the items you missed or looked up. When you finish, tap Back to Game. You return to the belt with 3 stars.

    8. On the receipt at the end, tap Play Again, Practice Missed (n) or Done.

    What to do next

    After about 15 minutes of play, a sheet suggests a stopping point. Tap Keep going or Take a break. In Practice mode the belt has no stars and no Break Time, and the item's name is shown.
