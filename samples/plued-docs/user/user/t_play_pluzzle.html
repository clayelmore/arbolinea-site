    Task

    Play the daily PLUzzle

    PLUzzle shows one produce item a day, the same for every player. You have 6 guesses to find its 4-digit code. It is free for everyone.

    Procedure

    1. On Home, tap the PLUzzle row at the top.
      Today's item appears with Guess the PLU.
    2. Type a 4-digit code on the keypad.

      Any 4-digit number is a valid guess.

    3. Tap the check mark key.
      Each digit of your guess changes color:
      • Green: the right digit in the right place.
      • Yellow: a digit in the code, in another place.
      • Tan: a digit that is not in the code.
    4. Repeat steps 2 and 3 until you find the code or use all 6 guesses.
      The result sheet opens with your streak and stats.
    5. Optional: Tap Share Results.

      The shared text has the puzzle number, your score, a link to the puzzle and a grid of colored squares. It does not show the code.

    6. Optional: Tap Practice This Produce to drill today's item.

      Practice on an item outside Level 1 needs the full app.

    What to do next

    A new puzzle comes each day. The PLUzzle row on Home shows whether you played today and your current streak.
