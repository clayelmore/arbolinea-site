    Contents

    PLUed User help: every topic in this build, in reading order.

    • About PLUedPLUed is a training app for grocery cashiers. It teaches the 4-digit PLU codes that cashiers type at the register for fresh produce.
      • How PLU codes workA PLU code is a 4-digit number that identifies a fresh produce item at the register. The International Federation for Produce Standards (IFPS) assigns the standard codes.
      • Who uses PLUedCashiers learn the codes. Shoppers look up produce and its nutrition. Store managers and trainers prepare their store's codes for the cashiers.
    • User Guide
      • Get started
        • Set up PLUed at first launchThe first time you open the app, it asks whether you work a register or are shopping. Cashiers then pick a region and an experience level and can load their store's codes.
        • The concept cardA concept card shows one kind of produce and every code it owns. You open it from the Library, from a scan, or from a card on Home.
        • Look up a PLU codeSearch the Library by name, code or other name to find an item and open its concept card.
        • Scan a produce stickerPoint the camera at a PLU sticker to open the item's concept card. The scanner reads the code on the phone, and no picture leaves the device.
      • Study and practice
        • Choose the codes you studyEvery code has a star. Turn a star off for a code your store does not use, and the code leaves your flashcards, games and mastery count.
        • Study with flashcardsEach flashcard is one kind of produce, with a slot for each of its codes. You type the codes from memory, and the app brings each card back when you are about to forget it.
        • Play Quick Play on the beltIn Quick Play, produce rides a checkout belt and you type each code before the item reaches the end. You earn and lose stars as you go.
        • Stars in Quick PlayEach Quick Play session starts with 5 stars. This table lists how each answer changes your stars.
        • Play the daily PLUzzlePLUzzle shows one produce item a day, the same for every player. You have 6 guesses to find its 4-digit code. It is free for everyone.
        • The Density ScoreThe Density Score is a number from 0 to 99 that says how much nutrition a produce item gives compared with other produce. It is free for everyone.
      • Your store
        • Import your store's packA store pack holds a store's own codes, its practice lists and the pictures for each item. Import the pack your trainer shared to study your store's codes.
        • Unlock the app with a store codeIf your store set up PLUed for its cashiers, it has one store code for everyone. Enter it once to unlock the whole app on your phone.
      • Reference
        • SettingsTo open Settings, tap the gear icon on Home. This table lists every group and what it does, from top to bottom.
        • Free and full accessPart of PLUed is free for everyone. A one-time purchase, or a store code from your store, unlocks the rest. There is no subscription.
        • TroubleshootingThis table lists problems cashiers and shoppers report, what causes them and how to fix them. Quoted text is what the app shows.
