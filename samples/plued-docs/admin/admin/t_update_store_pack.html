    Update a store pack

    Reopen a pack you already shared, change it, and share it again. The pack keeps its link and QR code, so printed QR codes keep working.

    Before you begin

    One of these:

    • The browser you built the pack in.
    • The pack's share link.
    • The pack's .plupack file.

    Procedure

    1. In a web browser, open plued.app/pack.
      The Store Pack Builder opens. It runs in the browser and needs no account.
    2. Decide whether the builder shows your pack.

      In the browser you built the pack in, the pack reopens by itself.

      IfThen
      Your pack is open Continue with step 5.
      The builder is empty or shows another pack Continue with step 3.
    3. Under Already have a pack?, tap Open from share link.

      If you have the file instead of the link, tap Open a .plupack file, choose the file, and continue with step 5.

    4. Paste the share link and tap Open.
      The builder opens the pack with its codes, practice lists and pictures.
    5. Make your changes.
      • To add a code, tap Add code.
      • To remove a code, tap the Delete row button (an x) beside the row's Size menu. The code also leaves every practice list it was in.
      • Change names, pictures, sections and practice lists as you did when you built the pack.
    6. Open step 4, Share with your team, and wait while the share button reads Publishing….
      The pack publishes again under the same link and QR code.
    7. Ask your cashiers to open Library > Import Pack and tap Update available beside your pack.

      A pack update does not reach the phones by itself. A store logo is the exception: a logo added later arrives without a tap.

    Results

    After a cashier taps Update available, the codes you added are on the phone and the codes you removed are gone from it. The cashier's practice history stays. A code that a cashier removed on the phone stays removed when the pack updates.

    What to do next

    CAUTION:
    Start over is for a different store. It clears the builder, and the next pack you share gets a new link. A pack you already shared stays shared.
