    Share a store pack

    Publish the pack from step 4 of the Store Pack Builder to get its share link and QR code. Cashiers open the link or scan the code to add the pack to their app.

    Before you begin

    • The pack is open in the Store Pack Builder and step 4 reads Ready to share.
    • An internet connection.

    About this task

    Every shared pack sends a copy to PLUed, which checks it before its link appears. The check reads every name in the pack, in every language.

    Procedure

    1. Open step 4, Share with your team.
      The share button reads Publishing… while the pack is checked.
    2. Decide what step 4 shows when the check ends.
      IfThen
      A QR code and Copy share link Continue with step 5.
      One name needs a small change before your pack can be shared., a message about a few names, or One name is too long for the app. Continue with step 3.
      Couldn't check the pack just now, so it can't be shared yet. Wait a minute, tap Retry, and repeat this step.
    3. Tap Go to it.
      The builder opens the name, with a short note under it such as Please try a different name here. or Please shorten this name.
    4. Type a different or shorter name, and go back to step 4.

      The check runs again by itself. If you think the name was fine, tap Let us know. under the message.

      The message goes away, and the QR code and Copy share link appear.
    5. Tap Copy share link and send the link to your cashiers by text or email.
      The builder shows Share link copied. The link starts with plued.app/p/.
    6. Optional: Show the QR code on your screen, or take a screenshot of it and print it for the break room.
    7. Tap Download .plupack and keep the file as a backup.

    Results

    A cashier scans the QR code with the phone's camera or taps the link, and the app imports the pack. In the app, the cashier can also open Library > Import Pack and tap Scan QR, paste the link and tap Import, or tap Open .plupack File. The app then shows Store Pack imported with the number of codes and your store's name.

    Anyone with the link can open the pack, so treat the QR code like a handout.
