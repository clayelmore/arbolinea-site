    Concept

    Store packs

    A store pack puts one store's own produce codes into its cashiers' copies of PLUed. You build it in the Store Pack Builder, a free page that runs in a web browser.

    A pack is optional. PLUed teaches the standard produce codes with no pack at all. A pack adds the local layer: the sizes your store stocks, the codes only your register uses, and the drills you want new hires to start with.

    What a pack carries

    Store name
    The name cashiers see on the store's card in the app.
    Codes
    Each code with its item name, its category (fruit, vegetable or other) and, when it matters, its size. A code can be a standard PLU or one of the store's own codes of 3 to 5 digits.
    Sections
    The aisle each code belongs to, such as Citrus or a section you name yourself.
    Practice lists
    Optional groups of items to drill together, such as a front table or a new hire's first week.
    Pictures
    One picture from the PLUed catalog for each code. You never upload a picture. For an item the catalog does not show, you pick the closest picture and can request a new one.

    A pack can also carry a second name for the store, each code, each section and each practice list, so that a cashier whose phone is set to that language sees those names.

    The builder

    The Store Pack Builder opens at plued.app/pack. It is free and needs no account. It keeps your draft in the browser you work in, so the pack is still there when you come back on the same computer. The builder walks you through four steps: Name your store, Add your codes, Practice lists and Share with your team.

    How cashiers receive a pack

    When you share a pack, the builder gives you a link and a QR code. A cashier scans the QR code with the phone's camera or taps the link, and the app imports the pack. A cashier who is already in the app can open Library > Import Pack and scan the QR code, paste the link, or open a .plupack file. The phone needs a connection the first time.

    After the import, the cashier has a card on Home titled with your store's name, your items in the Library under My Store, and your practice lists as named collections under your store's name. The standard codes stay in the app beside yours.

    Any cashier can import a pack and look through it for free. Practicing it needs the cashier's one-time unlock or your store's code from a Store Plan.

    Note:
    Level 1, PLUzzle, the Density Score and the nutrition tools are free. Other study needs the one-time unlock or a store code.
