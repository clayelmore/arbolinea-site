    Request your store's logo

    Type your store's website in the Store Pack Builder, and PLUed adds the store's logo to your pack. The request is free, and you never upload a picture.

    Before you begin

    • A store pack in the Store Pack Builder.
    • Your store's own website, with the logo on it. PLUed adds only a logo your store may use.

    Procedure

    1. In a web browser, open plued.app/pack.
      The Store Pack Builder opens. It runs in the browser and needs no account.
    2. On step 1, Name your store, find Want your store’s logo in the app? (optional) under the store name.
    3. Type your store's website, for example www.example.com.
    4. Share the pack from step 4, Share with your team.
      The request is sent with the pack.
    5. After a few days, open your pack in the builder again and read the line under the website box.
      If the line saysThen
      We got your request. We check each website ourselves, so allow a few days. The request is still waiting. Check again later.
      Your logo is in the app. The logo is shown small under the line. Nothing more to do.
      A note from PLUed: Read the note. It says what is needed from you.
      Your store sets its logo on its Store Plan status page. Your store has a Store Plan. Upload the logo on your store page instead.

    Results

    The logo shows on your store's card on Home in every cashier's app, beside your store's name on the Import Store Pack screen, and on your store's chip in the Library for a cashier with two or more stores. A wide logo that spells out the store's name runs across the top of a wider card on Home.

    Cashiers who already have your pack get the logo by itself, usually within a day. They do not need to tap Update available.
