    About PLUed

    PLUed is a training app for grocery cashiers. It teaches the 4-digit PLU codes that cashiers type at the register for fresh produce.

    Each produce item in PLUed has a picture, its name and its codes. You study with flashcards and practice on a moving checkout belt. The app starts with the items a store rings most often and adds the rest over time.

    What the app holds

    PLUed 1.2.3 holds 1,116 produce items, grouped into 899 produce concepts. A concept is one kind of produce, such as Hass avocados. One concept can own several codes for different sizes, regions or growing methods.

    Where it runs

    The app runs on iPhone and iPad, on Android phones and tablets, and on Amazon Fire tablets. A web demo runs in a browser at plued.app/demo. The web demo has the free tier only, with no camera and no pack import.

    About this help

    This help has these guides:

    • The User Guide, for cashiers and shoppers.
    • The Admin Guide, for store managers and trainers who prepare a store's codes.
    • How PLU codes work
      A PLU code is a 4-digit number that identifies a fresh produce item at the register. The International Federation for Produce Standards (IFPS) assigns the standard codes.
    • Who uses PLUed
      Cashiers learn the codes. Shoppers look up produce and its nutrition. Store managers and trainers prepare their store's codes for the cashiers.
