    Free and full access

    Part of PLUed is free for everyone. A one-time purchase, or a store code from your store, unlocks the rest. There is no subscription.

    Table 1. What each tier includes
    FeatureFreeFull
    Level 1: study, practice and playYesYes
    PLUzzleYesYes
    Density Score and the nutrition explorerYesYes
    Browsing the Library and every concept cardYesYes
    Scanning a stickerYesYes
    Looking at your store's packYesYes
    Levels 2 to 10, store sections and recipe setsShown, lockedYes
    Flashcards and games on every itemNoYes
    Practicing your store's packNoYes

    Unlocking

    Tap a locked level, or tap Unlock everything under Market access in Settings. The Unlock the whole market sheet shows the price from your app store. The sheet also opens by itself when you finish Level 1.

    If the app store does not answer, the sheet shows Store unavailable. Check your connection and tap Retry.

    Restore purchases

    If you bought the full app on another device with the same app store account, tap Restore purchases on the unlock sheet or in Settings.
