    Concept

    The Density Score

    The Density Score is a number from 0 to 99 that says how much nutrition a produce item gives compared with other produce. It is free for everyone.

    Where you see it

    The score sits under the item's name on its concept card. Tap the score for the full breakdown, or tap the information icon beside it for a short explanation. The explanation is stored in the app and opens without a connection. In the Library, the Nutrition view orders every item by its score.

    What goes into it

    Table 1. Parts of the score
    PartWhat it measures
    Nutrient concentration, 50%How packed the food is with nutrients once the water is set aside. More sugar lowers it.
    Nutrition per serving, 30%How much nutrition a typical serving gives, as % Daily Value.
    Low calories, 10%Fewer calories per bite scores higher.
    Plant compounds, 10%Natural plant compounds beyond vitamins.

    The four bands

    Powerhouse
    75 to 99
    Strong
    50 to 74
    Everyday
    25 to 49
    Treat
    Under 25

    A lower score means less nutrition per bite. It never means that a food is bad.

    Items with no score

    A few items have no score, such as decorative items, wrapper leaves and store codes the app has no data for. For these the card shows a line in place of the score, for example No Density Score with the reason. The app never shows a missing score as 0.

    Note:
    Level 1, PLUzzle, the Density Score and the nutrition tools are free. Other study needs the one-time unlock or a store code.
