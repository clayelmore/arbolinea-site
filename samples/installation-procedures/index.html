Installation procedures

Two installation procedures I wrote for telephone switching equipment. Installers follow them step by step in a working switch, so each one has safe stop points, the commands to enter and the responses to expect, and checks before moving on.

These are excerpts. Selected sections, figures, and one table are shown, and step, figure, and table numbers are kept from the full procedure. Company names, the product brand, and document numbers have been removed.
